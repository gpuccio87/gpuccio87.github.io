An automation for Claude that tidies up the mail of several accounts (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, Zoho) by working directly on the local Thunderbird profile. It follows a seven-step workflow: syncing, emptying the trash folders, fixing spam-filter false positives in both directions, unsubscribing from newsletters, emptying the spam folder, triaging the inbox with filters, tags and tasks created in Todoist, and finally cleaning up expired emails. It also answers questions like "find that email from..." or "who emails me the most".
