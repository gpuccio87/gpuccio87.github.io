Two automations for Claude that, together, help value a home. The first searches immobiliare.it for listings for sale or rent in an area, finds comparables and retrieves the average prices per square metre from the "mercato immobiliare" (property market) section. The second browses the public portal of the Property Market Observatory (OMI) of the Agenzia delle Entrate, the Italian Revenue Agency, and extracts the official OMI values by municipality, zone, half-year and cadastral category, including for historical comparisons. Cross-referencing the sources gives a reasoned estimate of the value.
