myNas is a private NAS built by recycling an Android mini PC (Minix Neo X7) and two 2TB HDDs. The project includes setting up all the network protocols for file sharing, backing files up onto the respective HDDs, an Android app to control and manage the files on the NAS, and a case designed and 3D printed to house and cool the mini PC and the HDDs.
