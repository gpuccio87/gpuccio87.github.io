CarWebhook is an Android script, built with Tasker and Javascript, that saves on the device, and sends to a webhook (so the data can be fed, for example, into a home automation system), the car's last parking spot and how many km were driven on the last trip. The script kicks in when the phone connects to and disconnects from the car's bluetooth system. When you park, a notification appears on your smartphone so you can find the parked car again. The kilometres driven are calculated using the OpenStreetMap API. This last piece of data is useful, for example, for calculating fuel consumption.
