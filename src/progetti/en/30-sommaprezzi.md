Sommaprezzi is a Chrome extension that works on Trovaprezzi, the Italian price-comparison site, and finds the shop where a basket of several products costs least, shipping included. No account, no data sent anywhere.

Trovaprezzi tells you where a single product is cheapest. But when you need three or four, at an online pharmacy for instance, the lowest price for each is almost always in a different shop, and every shop has its own shipping fee: sometimes free above a certain amount, sometimes not. Sommaprezzi does the sum on the whole order: you add products to a basket as you browse, with the quantity you need, and the top of the panel shows the best shop and the total.

If splitting the order between two or more shops is cheaper, it says so, with what to buy where and how much you save compared with the best single shop. This isn't an estimate: the extension tries every way of splitting the basket and takes free-shipping thresholds and minimum orders into account. When a search mixes different formats, a filter on the offer name (for example "compresse -supposte", tablets but not suppositories) tells it which offers to count.

The basket stays in your browser and the extension only runs on the Trovaprezzi pages you open yourself. Prices and shipping fees are the ones the shops declare: check the total in the shop's own basket before you pay. It's an independent project, not affiliated with Trovaprezzi.
