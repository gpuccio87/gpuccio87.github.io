The simple game of TicTacToe, or noughts and crosses, but played by an AI (<a class="ajax-page-load" href="#portfolio/portfolio-7.html">AnnA</a> or <a class="ajax-page-load" href="#portfolio/portfolio-8.html">Golem</a>). In Golem's case, the AI was selected through evolution by <a class="ajax-page-load" href="#portfolio/portfolio-9.html">Loew</a>, which picked the best neural network for this task. TicTacToe AI uses two competing neural networks, one for the right move and one for the wrong move.
