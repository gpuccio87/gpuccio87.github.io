Todoist Auto is a script for Google Assistant and Android, built with Tasker, Javascript, Actions on Google and the Todoist API. The script lets you invoke a voice Action with Google Assistant (on a smartphone, Wear OS, Android Auto, Google Home mini etc.) to add tasks to Todoist. Depending on the phrase, tasks are added to different projects or with different labels (for example "Remind me before I go out...", "Remind me when I get home...", "Remind me to call..."). In addition, when the phone connects to the car's bluetooth, the script reads aloud all the tasks under Todoist's "In the car" filter.
