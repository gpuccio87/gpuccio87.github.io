An automation for Claude that works on the Fattura24 invoicing software through its REST API. Just say or type "make an invoice for..." and Claude issues invoices, e-invoices sent through the SDI (the Italian e-invoicing exchange system), receipts, credit notes and daily takings records, adds customers to the customer register and downloads the PDF of issued documents, checking templates, chart of accounts and numbering series so that everything is filled in correctly.
