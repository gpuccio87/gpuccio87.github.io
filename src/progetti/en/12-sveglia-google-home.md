This alarm-clock-shaped dock was designed to hold a Google Home Mini, which fits snugly inside the dock and can then be used on a bedside table just like a real alarm clock. The dock is made up of 5 parts, printed separately and then assembled or screwed together. It was printed in eco-friendly PLA.
