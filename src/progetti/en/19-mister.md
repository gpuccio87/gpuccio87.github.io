The evolution of fantasy football. Mister! takes fantasy football a step further, beyond the classic game and beyond Mantra. It doesn't stop at the newspaper player ratings: it uses each player's real statistics, that is shots, passes, duels and tackles, and with these an engine simulates the match minute by minute.

That way the match really becomes yours. Every week you pick your eleven and set up the tactics board: who you put on the pitch, how you line them up, whether you press or sit back. Mister! plays it against your opponent's choices, and the result comes from what happens on the pitch, just like in real football.

You build your squad at an auction. After each matchday you'll find a match report and an analysis of what decided it. You can play with friends or in a public league, and for those who prefer tradition there's also the classic mode with ratings and bonuses.

It's a personal project still in development (alpha), online at <a href="https://mister.gabrielepucciarelli.com" target="_blank">mister.gabrielepucciarelli.com</a>.
