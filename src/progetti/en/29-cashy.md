Ca$hy is a Chrome extension that lets you know when the shop you're visiting offers cashback or discounted gift cards, and reminds you of the discount codes you've saved. No account, no data sent anywhere.

Cashback only works if you go through it before you buy, and a discount code only works if you can find it again. Ca$hy does both for you: open the shop's website and, if there's something to grab, a pill appears in a corner with the best offer. One click shows you everything, with cashback and gift cards in two separate sections (they're two different things) and the offers sorted from the best deal down.

The discount codes you get by email you save once, with their value and expiry date, and they reappear by themselves on the site they're for, ahead of any other offer. From seven days before the expiry date Ca$hy reminds you; the day after, it deletes the expired code by itself. Codes sync between your browsers through your Google account and are never sent to any server.

On sites without offers it runs no code at all, and it can be silenced for one page, for the session or for good on a given site. The catalogue has more than 3,150 shops, updated every 24 hours.
