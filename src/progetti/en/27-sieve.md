Sieve: the day's news, sifted. Hundreds of articles come out every day, and many of them tell the same story: Sieve reads them for you and tells you which ones really matter.

Every two hours it gathers articles from about ninety sources: news agencies, Italian newspapers and international outlets. Articles about the same event become a single fact, with all its sources, and every fact gets a weight from 0 to 10 that measures how much it matters, not how recent it is. At the top of the page is what carries weight, with a short summary and links to the original articles. Readers choose the threshold themselves: a chart shows how the day's weights are distributed. You can read it as a list or as a newspaper page, and filter by section.

Twice a day, morning and evening, Sieve also becomes a podcast a few minutes long with the main facts: you can listen on the site, in any podcast app through the RSS feed, or in the Telegram group, which also gets the news roundup.

No paywall, no ads, no cookies; the summaries are automatic, but the original article is always one click away. Sieve is free, runs on donations and is online at <a href="https://sieve.gabrielepucciarelli.com" target="_blank">sieve.gabrielepucciarelli.com</a>.
