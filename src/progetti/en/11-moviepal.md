MoviePal is a Python desktop application that suggests films and TV series. It started in 2021 as a simple script and today it's a complete program with a Qt graphical interface, available in six languages and distributed as a Windows executable.

Based on the titles the user has watched and the rating they gave each one, MoviePal builds a graph of connected films (using data from TMDB, OMDb and MDBList) and ranks suggestions by "affinity", combining several algorithms. It keeps track of what you've watched, what it has suggested and what you've discarded, has filters for genre, year, platform and rating, and two separate modes for films and series.

It connects to the services of your home media library: it reads and writes ratings on Plex and Jellyfin, and sends the titles you pick to Radarr and Sonarr to download them. MoviePal gave rise to <a class="ajax-page-load" href="portfolio-18.html">Tonight</a>, its multi-user web version. The name, "MoviePal", was generated by ChatGPT!
