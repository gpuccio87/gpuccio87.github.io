Anyone who plays fantasy football knows how it goes: on Monday you wait for the newspaper player ratings, add up the bonuses, and the result pops out of a sum. The real match, the one between my team and my opponent's, is never actually played.

That's where **Mister!** comes from: I wanted a fantasy football game where my choices counted on the pitch, not just on the scoresheet.

### Statistics instead of ratings

Mister! doesn't stop at the newspaper ratings. It uses each player's **real statistics**, that is shots, passes, duels and tackles, and with these an engine **simulates the match minute by minute**.

So the result isn't a sum: it comes from what happens on the pitch. A midfielder who wins lots of tackles in real life will win them in the simulation too; a striker who shoots a lot and scores little will get his chances, but there's no guarantee he'll take them.

### Really being the manager

Every week you pick your eleven and prepare the **tactics board**: who you put on the pitch, how you line them up, whether you press high or sit back. Mister! plays your line-up against your opponent's choices, and tactics matter: you don't play against a passing side the way you play against one that goes long.

Helping you out there's the "scout", who studies your opponent and tells you how they play and where they struggle.

### After the final whistle

You build your squad at an **auction**, just like in classic fantasy football. After each matchday you'll find the **match report**, play by play, with the statistics and an analysis of what decided it. So when you lose 1-5 (it happens, believe me), at least you understand why.

You can play with friends or in a public league. And for those who prefer tradition there's also the classic mode, with ratings and bonuses.

### Where it stands

Mister! is a personal project still in development, in **alpha**: [mister.gabrielepucciarelli.com](https://mister.gabrielepucciarelli.com). If you're a fantasy football manager and you'd like to try it, drop me a line.
