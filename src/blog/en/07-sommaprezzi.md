Trovaprezzi, the Italian price-comparison site, is great for finding where one product is cheapest. The trouble starts when you need three or four, as often happens at an online pharmacy: the lowest price for each is almost always in a different shop, and every shop has its own shipping fee. Sometimes it's free above a certain amount, sometimes it isn't.

The sum you actually need is a different one: where do I spend least on **the whole order**, shipping included? **Sommaprezzi** was built to answer that question.

### A basket on top of Trovaprezzi

Sommaprezzi is an extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave. As you browse Trovaprezzi you add products to a basket, with the quantity you need; it reads every offer and the top of the panel shows **the best shop and the total**, worked out on the whole order: prices, shipping, free-shipping threshold, minimum order.

I saw how surprising the result can be on the very first try, with three pharmacy products. The shop with the lowest prices had €47.29 of goods, but with €5 shipping it came third. Another one, with goods at €50.13, shipped for free and won.

### One shop, two or more

Sometimes it pays to **split the order**: one product from one shop, the other two from another. Sommaprezzi says so, with what to buy where and how much you save compared with the best single shop.

This isn't an estimate: the extension tries every way of splitting the basket and keeps the cheapest combination. An extra shop is suggested only if it really saves money.

### Filters

A Trovaprezzi search can mix different formats: tablets and suppositories, packs of 10 and of 16. A filter on the offer name, for example `compresse -supposte` (tablets, not suppositories), tells the extension which offers to count. With a product code (minsan or EAN) you usually don't need one.

### No tricks

Sommaprezzi collects nothing and sends nothing: the basket stays in your browser. Prices and shipping fees are the ones the shops declare to Trovaprezzi, so check the total in the shop's own basket before you pay. It's free, independent and not affiliated with Trovaprezzi.
